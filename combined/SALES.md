# PARA Flow — Capture at the Speed of Thought. Ship What Matters.

## Two rivals built it. Neither would trade it back.

PARA Flow is what happened when two competing PARA apps were forced into one room: Muse's disciplined, offline, inbox-driven **PARA Life OS** and Gemini's fast, keyboard-first **PARA Flow OS** — critiqued by each other, merged on the merits, every surviving idea credited in a public change log. What survived is stronger than either entry alone.

Four homes for everything, sorted by the only question that matters — **"How actionable is this, right now?"**

**◆ Projects** — with a goal, a deadline, a next action, and the Area they serve.
**⬢ Areas** — with a standard you can actually check.
**⬣ Resources** — handy, out of the way, one click from promotion.
**▭ Archives** — finished or paused, stamped with why, restorable to exactly where they lived.

## Why this one wins

**Capture with zero friction — decide when you're ready.** Quick Capture defaults to the Inbox, a decision queue you process to zero. Already know where it belongs? Pick the tier right in the capture box. No other organiser offers both speeds.

**A command palette for your whole life.** Ctrl/⌘+K. Every item, every command — new capture, export, the Weekly Review — three keystrokes away. Your second brain at keyboard speed.

**Moves that mean something.** A Resource doesn't get "filed" — it gets **Promoted to Project**. A Project doesn't get "moved" — it gets **Completed & Archived**, tasks checked off, history stamped. PARA Flow's transitions celebrate the milestones other apps treat as filing chores.

**A Weekly Review that walks with you.** Not a checklist you'll learn to ignore — a guided wizard: clear the Inbox decision by decision, audit every Project's next action, check every Area's standard, archive the dormant. Fifteen minutes, and the system is trustworthy again.

**Projects with a lineage.** Every Project can name the Area it serves — because projects end, but responsibilities don't, and seeing that line is how you stop confusing the two (Tiago Forte's core warning, built into the data model).

**Yours. Private. Instant. Unkillable.** No account, no subscription, no CDN, no server. One file, fully offline, forever. Your data lives in your browser and exports *and imports* in one click.
