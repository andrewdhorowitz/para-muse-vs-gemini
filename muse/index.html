The PARA rule

Projects — active, with a goal + deadline.
Areas — ongoing, with a standard to maintain.
Resources — topics you care about, for later.
Archives — inactive, out of sight, never deleted.

Ask: “How actionable is this, right now?” That answer is its home.

Your life, sorted by what needs you next.
