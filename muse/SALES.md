# PARA Life OS — Your Life, Sorted by What Needs You Next

## Stop filing. Start finishing.

Every notes app, drive folder, and to-do list you've ever tried asked you the wrong question: *"Where does this belong?"*

PARA Life OS asks the only question that actually gets things done: **"How actionable is this — right now?"**

Four answers. Four homes. Nothing else to learn.

**◆ Projects** — what you're actively finishing, with a goal and a deadline.
**⬢ Areas** — what you're maintaining, with a standard you can actually check.
**⬣ Resources** — what you care about, kept handy and out of the way.
**▭ Archives** — what's done or dormant, gone from view, never lost.

That's Tiago Forte's PARA method — and this is the app that doesn't just store it, it *runs* it.

## Why this one wins

**Capture in 3 seconds, decide when you're ready.** The Quick Capture box is always on screen. Dump the thought, it lands in the Inbox, your brain lets go of it. Later, one click per item processes the Inbox to zero. No other organiser makes the capture → clarify → organise loop this tight.

**Deadlines that tap you on the shoulder.** The dashboard leads with what's overdue and what's due in the next two weeks — not a wall of undifferentiated cards. Open the app, know your focus. That's it. That's the morning briefing most people pay an assistant for.

**Projects that prove they're projects.** Every Project carries its goal, its deadline, its *next physical action*, a progress bar, and a checklist. If it doesn't have those, the app quietly shows you — and you either sharpen it or move it to Areas or Resources where it honestly belongs. Vague lists become real plans.

**Reorganising is one click, because life reorganises constantly.** A Resource becomes a Project the day you get serious. A Project becomes an Archive the day you ship it — or pause it. In PARA Life OS, moving anything is a single dropdown on its card. Competing apps bury that in drag-and-drop rituals and settings pages. Here, the PARA move *is* the interface.

**Yours. Private. Instant. Free to run.** No account. No subscription. No server holding your life's operating system hostage. Open `index.html` or the live link and you're in — your data lives in your browser, exports in one click, and the app works offline, forever, even if every cloud service on earth goes down tomorrow.

**Review built in, not bolted on.** A Weekly Review checklist lives on the dashboard: empty the Inbox, check every Project's next action, glance at your Areas' standards, archive what's finished, promote what earned it. Fifteen minutes a week and the system stays trustworthy — which is the whole secret of getting things done.

## Who it's for

The founder juggling launches and family. The student with five classes and a side project. Anyone with 400 notes, 9 apps, and the nagging feeling that something important is buried somewhere. If your digital life feels like a junk drawer, PARA Life OS turns it into four clean trays — and tells you which tray needs you today.
